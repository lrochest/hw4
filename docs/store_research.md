## Policies
- **Returns:** 30 days from the shipping date. Items must be unworn/unused with original
  tags and labels.
- **Return shipping:** store pays if it was the store's error; otherwise the customer pays
  (insured, trackable shipping recommended).
- **Refunds:** exclude original shipping costs; 2–10 business days to appear, depending on bank.
- **Final sale:** custom-made items (e.g. Custom Alumni products) cannot be returned or exchanged.
- **Production:** most orders produced in 5–8 business days, then shipped.
- **Shipping:** most domestic orders via UPS; ships worldwide; the customer pays customs duties
  and import taxes. Tracking email sent on shipment. Weather, holidays and carriers can cause delays.
- Not stated on the site: shipping costs, free-shipping threshold, in-store pickup. The agent
  should say it doesn't know these, not make them up.
